01 About
From business problems to intelligent systems.

Sharjeel combines a background in business analysis with hands-on experience in AI automation and agentic AI engineering. That mix means he doesn't just build automations — he understands the business process underneath them, and designs systems that solve the actual problem.

His work spans workflow automation and CRM automation using tools like n8n, Make.com, Zapier, and HighLevel, along with API integrations that connect disconnected tools into a single automated system. On the AI side, he builds with Python and LLM-based systems, developing AI agents that can reason, use tools, and carry out multi-step tasks.

He's actively building and refining these systems — applying an agentic approach to automation so it doesn't just run on rails, but can adapt, decide, and act.
